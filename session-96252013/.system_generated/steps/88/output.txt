In *Grand Theft Auto VI* (*GTA 6*), the story campaign following protagonists Jason and Lucia across the state of Leonida is divided into **5 main chapters**[1] encompassing roughly 90–99 total story missions[2][3]. 

The structural breakdown of the chapters and their respective themes/titles include:

### **Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise**[4]
* **Focus:** Serves as the extended opening block of the game (featuring around 22–23 missions, including the Prologue)[5][6], introducing players to the mechanics, the regional setting outside the city, and characters like Jason, Lucia, and their early criminal associates as they pull off local smuggling and odd jobs[5][7].
* **Notable early missions include:** *Prologue*, *Gone Fishing*, *Gear Up*, *Just A Scratch*, *Lab Fees*, *Unwanted Attention*, and *Buck Me*[5][7].

### **Chapter 2: Paradise Lost**[4]
* **Focus:** Consists of roughly 11 missions[6]. Escalates the friction in Jason and Lucia's criminal operations, forcing them to adapt as regional law enforcement and rival elements close in, pushing them deeper into the underbelly of Leonida.

### **Chapter 3: Tits Ass Drug Money**[4]
* **Focus:** One of the larger blocks in the game with around 26 missions[6]. This chapter ramps up the scale of smuggling, larger heists, and high-stakes operations as the protagonists expand their influence and network across different territories.

### **Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby**[4]
* **Focus:** Comprising about 25 missions[6], this chapter shifts the core of the narrative directly into the neon-lit urban sprawl of Vice City[8]. Operations become more organized, moving toward major corporate targets, high-end scores, and complex webbed connections.

### **Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma**[4]
* **Focus:** The finale chapter (around 7 missions)[6] acting as the grand climax. It brings together the overarching plots, the fallout of previous choices, and the final sequence of high-risk missions determining Jason and Lucia's ultimate fate[9].

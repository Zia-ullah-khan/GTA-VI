The search for ""Sinfrontera National Bank" "The Cartel Heist" setup missions GTA 6" returned the following summary:
In *Grand Theft Auto VI*, **"The Cartel Heist"** is the climactic final mission of **Chapter 3**, where Jason and Lucia team up with Raul Bautista to rob the **Sinfrontera National Bank** in Tequesta (Vice City area), which is used by the Colombian cartel *La Polilla Negra* to launder money[1][2][3].

Successfully executing a "perfect run" and maximizing your payout (up to a $10.5 million take) depends heavily on completing a series of mandatory and optional setup/preparation missions throughout Chapter 3[4][5]. 

The setup and preparation missions required before hitting the Sinfrontera National Bank include:

### 1. Sinfrontera National Bank (Scope Out)
* **Mission Giver:** Raul Bautista[2]
* **Objective:** Jason and Lucia, alongside Raul and Andrea, secretly case the bank to inspect security systems, guard patrols, and camera layouts[1][2]. This kicks off the planning phase for the operation.

### 2. Security Expert
* **Objective:** Track down and retrieve **Nicole Park**, the bank's internal security expert who has the knowledge required to bypass the high-end vault systems[4][1]. Securing her cooperation is crucial for a smoother breach.

### 3. Company Property
* **Objective:** Meet up with Pete to acquire the heavy-duty explosives and specialized gear needed to breach the bank's secure infrastructure[4][6]. After receiving the gear, you must defend the haul from an ambush by opposing cops/rivals to ensure *All Cargo Protected*[4].

### 4. The Missing Link
* **Objective:** Hunt down, capture, and interrogate **Monty** to squeeze vital insider intelligence out of him regarding the cartel's bank schedules and operational flaws[4]. Getting *all* information from Monty directly impacts how well you can optimize the heist.

### 5. Safe Word
* **Objective:** Steal a practice safe and bring it back so that Nicole can train Jason and Lucia on it[4]. Successfully completing this prep translates to faster, more efficient safe-cracking during the actual heist.

### 6. Hidden Charges
* **Objective:** Plant remote explosives at the base of multiple regional cell towers (**East, South, and West Cell Towers**)[4]. 
* **Why it matters:** Sabotaging these communication towers plays a major role in delaying the police response time when you finally trigger the alarm during the main heist.

---

### How Prep Work Impacts "The Cartel Heist"
Completing these setup missions directly affects your operational window during the actual robbery at the Sinfrontera National Bank[4]:
* **Extended Timer / Slower Police Response:** Successfully sabotaging the cell towers and completing all auxiliary objectives cuts off police communications, granting you a significantly larger window (up to nearly 11 minutes before full police heat hits, and maximum seconds inside the vault) to grab maximum valuables before the authorities swarm the location[4][7][8]. 
* **Crew Survival:** Optimal preparation keeps characters like Nicole safe during the chaotic extraction phase[5].

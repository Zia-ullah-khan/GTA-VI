**"Protective Custody"** and **"The End of the Road"** are the final two back-to-back major story missions that comprise the climax and game-ending sequence of **Grand Theft Auto VI (GTA 6)**[1][2]. 

### 1. "Protective Custody" (Mission #108)
* **Context:** Triggered during Chapter 5[1][3] right after meeting with Abe Gonzalez (where the game prompts you to choose whether to *Proceed* into the finale or *Postpone* to wrap up open-world tasks)[4]. 
* **Plot:** Giorgio Gallo betrays Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, resulting in them being separately taken into custody[3]. Statewide prosecutor Danica Howard pressures Lucia, while both protagonists must separately survive ambushes by dirty cops, fend off a NOOSE assault, and take down Méndez's task force in a heavy firefight[3][5].
* **Key Decision:** At the end of the mission, players face critical choices involving Méndez and whether to take his deal, which directly feeds into how the narrative's branching conclusions play out[6].

### 2. "The End of the Road" (Mission #109)
* **Context:** The ultimate final main mission of GTA 6, which starts immediately following the completion of *Protective Custody*[7]. 
* **Plot:** Jason and Lucia stage a high-stakes final assault on Giorgio Gallo’s penthouse to retrieve a vital hard drive and fight for their freedom[7]. It features aerial turret sections, breaching the penthouse, fighting through waves of security, escaping helicopter pursuits, and heading toward the Keys[7].
* **The Final Outcomes:** Depending on cumulative gameplay factors—such as Jason and Lucia's relationship status ("Hot Together"), accumulated family savings (such as hitting the $2 million mark), criminal profiles, and prompt choices during the finale (like choosing *Swim For It*)—this mission leads to the game's credits and resolves the story into one of several distinct endings[8][9][6].

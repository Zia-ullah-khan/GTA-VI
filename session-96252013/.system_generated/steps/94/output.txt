The search for ""Operation Savage Glory" "Protective Custody" "Top of the World, Ma!" GTA 6 final mission" returned the following summary:
These terms are all tied to the final stretch and endgame of **Grand Theft Auto VI** (Chapter 5)[1][2]. 

Here is how they connect to the climax of the game:

* **"Top of the World, Ma!"**: This is the title of **Chapter 5** of the GTA 6 campaign, which takes place in Vice City and acts as the final narrative arc closing out Jason and Lucia's story[2][3]. 
* **"Operation Savage Glory"**: This is a major, roughly 50-minute multi-tiered tactical mission late in Chapter 5 (following missions like *Doggie Bag* and *VC Baby*)[4][1]. In it, Jason and Lucia work with Giorgio Gallo’s crew to raid a coast guard base, blow up bridge pillars, and recover a seized shipment[4]. Successfully completing it clears their immediate Leonida heat and rewards you with the *Snow Storm* achievement/trophy, setting up the final sequence of events[4][1].
* **"Protective Custody"**: This serves as the true point-of-return endgame trigger mission in Chapter 5[1][5]. After meeting with their lawyer Abe to finalize a pardon/deal, Giorgio betrays them, leading to separate ambushes, corrupt cops, a precinct shootout, and a critical confrontation with Méndez's task force[5][6]. 
  * *Note:* Entering Abe's office prompts a binary choice to **"Proceed"** or **"Postpone"**[7]. Choosing *Proceed* locks you into a nearly hour-long back-to-back finale sequence (*The End of the Road*) where free-roam is temporarily disabled until the credits roll[7][1]. Meeting specific financial prerequisites (such as banking $2 million in Family Savings) before locking into this final mission is tied to unlocking alternative endings[7][8].

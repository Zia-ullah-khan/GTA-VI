These are the titles of the five story chapters in ***Grand Theft Auto VI***, outlining the campaign progression for Jason and Lucia[1]:

1. **Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise** – Set primarily in the Leonida Keys[2][3].
2. **Chapter 2: Paradise Lost** – Set around Port Gellhorn[2][4].
3. **Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money** – The characters' arrival and establishment in Vice City[2][5].
4. **Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby** – Deepening criminal operations within Vice City[2][5].
5. **Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma!** – The game's final story chapter and finale[1][5].

### 1. What is "Cyberleek"?
"Cyberleek" is the moniker associated with a high-profile leaker (or group) tied to circulating deep development assets, gameplay footage, and internal files regarding *GTA 6*[1][2]. While initial massive leaks hit the web years prior, Cyberleek made headlines around the game's launch window by releasing extensive unedited gameplay, debug information, and internal build components (such as executable files and string data from leaked Xbox/debug developer builds)[2][3].

### 2. Mission Flows, `story000`, and Internal Nomenclature

The campaign structure of ***Grand Theft Auto VI*** spans **91 mandatory main story missions** (plus an opening Prologue), organized across **five distinct chapters**[1]. The game scales significantly in length compared to *GTA V* (closer to the scope of *Red Dead Redemption 2*), taking players from the Leonida Keys up through Port Gellhorn and into Vice City[1][2].

---

### **Important Mission Structure Changes**
* **No Gold Medals:** Rockstar has dropped the traditional performance-tracking system (time limits, accuracy quotas) seen in *GTA V* and *RDR2*[1].
* **No Mission Replay:** Unlike previous titles, you **cannot replay completed missions** from a menu. If you want to experience a specific heist or mission again, you must rely on manual save files[1].
* **Character Switching:** Early chapters focus primarily on individual protagonists (Jason or Lucia), but character-switching opens up dynamically during Chapter 2[1].

#### **Chapter 2: Paradise Lost**
* *Location:* Port Gellhorn / Starlet Motel area[5]
* *Overview:* Introduces wider free-roam character switching between Jason and Lucia[1][5]. Deals with expanding criminal operations outside the immediate keys.

#### **Chapter 3: Tits, Ass, Drug Money**
* *Location:* Transitioning toward Vice City outskirts and regional connections[2]
* *Overview:* Mid-game escalation where smuggling rings turn into high-stakes drug operations, laying the groundwork for the migration into the main city[2].

#### **Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby**
* *Location:* Vice City proper[2]
* *Overview:* The largest chunk of the game. Opens up the neon-lit urban core, featuring major setups, elaborate heists, and navigating complex criminal conspiracies[1][2].

#### **Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma!**
* *Location:* Vice City / Finale locations[2]
* *Overview:* The high-stakes culmination of Jason and Lucia's arc, wrapping up loose ends, betrayals, and the ultimate climax of the campaign[1].

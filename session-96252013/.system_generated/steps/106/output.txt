In ***Grand Theft Auto VI***, the campaign structure is divided into distinct chapters—primarily **Chapter 1 (Set in the Leonida Keys)** and **Chapter 2 (Set around Port Gellhorn and Kelly County)**—before moving toward Vice City[1][2][3]. Because GTA VI does **not** feature a mission replay menu or chapter select, tracking mission unlocks, prerequisites, and hard cutoffs between Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 is vital[1][4][3].

---

### **Chapter 1: "Just Like Paradise" (Leonida Keys)**
* **Primary Focus:** Primarily introduces Jason and builds out the early smuggling and criminal web in the Keys[5][6]. 
* **Notable Mission Structure & Unlocks:**
  * **Prologue:** Lucia’s introductory sequence[6].
  * **Early Missions:** Operations like *Gone Fishing*, *Gear Up*, *Lab Fees*, and *Island Dreams* establish baseline cash flow and open up initial mechanics[6].
  * **Key Unlocks:** 
    * Completing ***Island Dreams*** unlocks the initial **Kenton Sets** / strand activities[7].
    * Side activities and optional tasks like *Get Shirley’s Rum*, *Skunk Apeology 101*, and text-based jobs from Cal become available during Chapter 1[8].
* **Chapter 1 Cutoff / Transition Warning:** 
  * The final missions of Chapter 1 lead directly into a forced location shift. Certain activities, text triggers (such as specific lines from Cal), and ambient character moments must be completed before starting the chapter's final transition mission (often referenced as *Tourist Trap* or the final narrative bottleneck)[2][9]. 
  * *Tip:* Players frequently manual-save right before the end of Chapter 1 because you cannot replay missions later[4][3].

---

### **Chapter 2: "Paradise Lost" (Port Gellhorn / Kelly County)**
* **Primary Focus:** Shifts focus heavily to Lucia as the duo lays low around Port Gellhorn, introducing survival mechanics, motel management, and new local contacts[10].
* **Notable Mission Structure & Unlocks:**
  * Begins with missions tracking the infamous bag (*What's in the Bag?*) and dealing with living expenses (*Overnight Charges*, *Just Moved In*)[10].
  * **Key Unlocks & Prerequisites:**
    * **Vehicle Fencing & Registration:** You unlock the ability to register your dream car and fence vehicles after completing the Chapter 2 main mission **Moses** (*Take a Vehicle to Moses*) and interacting with the Slim Jim mechanic[7][11].
    * **Free Character Switching:** Starting with the Chapter 2 main mission **"Coffee Break,"** the game permanently unlocks the ability to freely switch back and forth between Jason and Lucia in the open world by holding right on the D-pad[1].
    * **Business Ventures:** Port Gellhorn criminal operations and side ventures (such as *Kolt and Associates*) open up early in Chapter 2 for steady passive income[11].
    * **Meditation / Collectibles:** Certain ambient world unlocks, like tracking down specific Meditation spots for the *Positive Mental Attitude* requirements, can be safely started or cleaned up here if missed during Chapter 1's tighter bottlenecks[12].

---

### **Core Gameplay Rules to Keep in Mind:**
1. **No Mission Replay:** Just like *Red Dead Redemption 2*, once a mission from Chapter 1 or 2 is finished, it's locked to that playthrough save file[1][4]. 
2. **Missable Side Content:** Time-sensitive optional missions are marked with an hourglass icon; failing to tackle them before pushing the main narrative forward causes them to drop off the map[4]. 
3. **Manual Saves:** Always keep a master manual save file at the tail end of Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 if you intend to experience alternate choices without restarting the entire 60+ hour campaign[4][3].

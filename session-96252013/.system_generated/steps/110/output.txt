In **Grand Theft Auto VI**, **"VCIA Takedown"** is the major multi-stage airport heist culminating Chapter 4 of the story (taking place around the Vice City International Airport)[1][2]. 

To unlock and successfully trigger the **VCIA Takedown** setup missions and fulfill the **Chapter 4 prerequisites**, you need to work through a specific sequence of events:

### 1. Chapter 4 Progress & Prerequisites
VCIA Takedown is not available right at the start of Chapter 4 ("Vice City, Baby"). You must first establish operations at the Y Hotel in Ocean Beach/Vice Beach[3][2] and progress through the earlier narrative arc of the chapter. 

* **The Cash Requirement:** Partway through Chapter 4 (following missions like *Party Hard*), you will be required to raise and send $75,000 to Abe Gonzalez via BuckMe to keep the story moving[1].
* **Prerequisite Quests:** You must clear the preceding narrative string involving Dre’Quan (culminating in his quest **"Headliners"**) as well as standard introductory Ocean Beach operations[4][3].

### 2. VCIA Takedown Setup Missions
Like other massive heists in the game, the VCIA Takedown requires several preparation and intelligence-gathering tasks before the actual job can go live[5]:
* **Preparation Quests:** You will undertake various undercover tasks, security jobs, and equipment acquisitions assigned by characters like Raul[4] and Kordell[6].
* **"Fully Catered":** This is one of the mandatory preparation/setup tasks tied directly into readying the operation[7]. 
* **Starting Location:** Once all preparation quests and prerequisite character missions (like Headliners) are cleared, the mission officially triggers out of the **Stockyard** area in Vice City[4].

### Quick Tips for VCIA Takedown:
* **Missable Weapon:** During the final ground shootout of the heist (when you switch back to Jason to defend Andrea while she loads bags), keep an eye out on the ground for **Francisco’s LMG-6** and pick it up before getting into the van[1].
* **Trophy/Achievement:** Completing the VCIA Takedown rewards you with the **"Hi-Jacked"** trophy[7].
* **Post-Heist Window:** Finishing VCIA Takedown puts Chapter 4 into an open-world waiting period (*Tie Up Loose Ends*), giving you a brief window to roam, bank-rob, and finish side content before the final chapter transition[8].
